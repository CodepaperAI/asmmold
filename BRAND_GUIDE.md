# ASM Mold Removal Blog Brand Guide

This guide was inferred from the live ASM Mold Removal website and translated into the blog subdomain theme.

## Brand Signals

- Service promise: same-day Ontario response, certified remediation, warranty-backed completion.
- Visual proof: real remediation imagery, CMR certification badge, service guarantee, Google review asset.
- Tone: direct, practical, safety-focused, and local to Ontario.
- Primary CTA: call `647-451-0493` or book an inspection.

## Design Tokens

- Primary blue: `#0a65f0`
- Deep blue: `#073b91`
- Ink: `#161616`
- Paper: `#ffffff`
- Wash: `#f4f7fb`
- Gold accent: `#c9a85c`
- Body font: `Poppins`
- Heading font: `Adamina`
- Corner radius: `4px` to `6px`

## Layout Rules

- Keep the phone CTA visible in the top navigation.
- Use real mold remediation/project imagery as the first-viewport signal.
- Lead with practical article content, not generic marketing copy.
- Use square or low-radius controls to match the existing site.
- Preserve dark header, blue CTA buttons, uppercase navigation, and Adamina headings.
